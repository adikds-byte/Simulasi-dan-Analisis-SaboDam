Parameter Struktur Sabo Dam (Given):

Tubuh utama Sabo Dam trapesium berhulu tegak memiliki lebar mercu 0.60 m, lebar dasar 4.20 m, tinggi 7.0 m, air hulu 6.0 m, endapan sedimen hs = 4.0 m, dan kecepatan aliran lahar vd = 3.0 m/s.

1 Gaya Dorong Air (Pw), Sedimen (Ps) & Lahar (Fd)
Pw = γw·h̄·A = 9.81(3)(6) = 176.58 kN
Ps = ½·Ka·γsub·hs² = 26.67 kN
Fd = kd·ρd·hd·vd² = 24.30 kN
ΣH = 227.55 kN (Mode Air Murni Foto: P = 176.58 kN / tertulis 175.58 kN)
2 Momen Penahan Guling (RM) terhadap Toe
W₁ = 0.6 × 7 × 24 = 100.80 kN (x₁ = 3.90 m)
W₂ = [7 × 3.6 / 2] × 24 = 302.40 kN (x₂ = 2.40 m)
RM = 100.8(3.90) + 302.4(2.40)
RM = 1118.90 kN.m (Nilai eksak: 393.12 + 725.76 = 1118.88 kN.m)
3 Factor of Safety Against Overturning (FS)
OM = 353.16 (Air) + 35.56 (Sed) + 115.43 (Debris)
FS = RM / OM = 1118.90 / 504.15 = 2.22
FS = 2.22
